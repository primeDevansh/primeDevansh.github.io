# What is success?

> "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take." - Anonymous
{: .prompt-tip}

'Success' is a subjective term, having different meanings for different persons. A roadside fruitseller may consider themselves successful while at the same time, the richest man in the world might not. Quite ironical. So, do we have any 'one size fits all' kind of deinition for success? Do we have a pathway to achieve success? Well, I feel elated to answer yes! Read more to find out.

Success, according to me, is the feeling one gets after achieving their goals. So, if a debt-ridden farmer's goal is to pay all of his debts and start their own business, it is success, which could've been the case with our fruitseller. On the other hand, if the richest person's goal is set their feet on a planet like Mars, they might not consider them successful. After all, it's always the perspective. Same things can carry different meanings when viewed from different perspectives. Same is the case with success; everybody has their own definition of success.

So, it is clear that having a goal is important to even think of success. However, we generally commit one common mistake; we set ourselves too big of a goal. I'm not saying it is bad; we should, rather, we must compulsorily have a mission for our lives but, we must make sure to segregate our goals into smaller, more achievable chunks. Chunks that can be conquered daily[^footnote1] - conquered, so that we have a consistent source of hope. So that we can always see light at the end of the tunnel - tunnel that leads us to the goals of our lives. Internalize this.

Now the obvious question is where should one begin? Simple. Gratitude is the first step towards success. I'll not digress much. Acknowledge what you've got, find the mission of your life, divide into smaller chunks and dive deep into the work required to achieve your goal. Take control.

When one invests themselves into work, they will often come across things which would be tempting. This often results in confusion. Confusion is dangerous. Confusion will consume your valuable time. You cannot afford to waste as valuable a resource like time. My solution. Simple, yet again. Visualisation is the key to solve most of your life's problems. Imagine yourself in a car on a one-way road that leads you to a destination. The destination is the ultimate mission of your life. Now, if the thing that intimidates you helps you in reaching your destination, it is for you (e.g., daily hoops practice if your mission is to make it to the NBA). On the other hand, if entertaining something sucks the life out of you and is making your journey harder, it isn't for you (e.g., smoking).

Before closing, I'd like to shed light on one more point, ten people will have ten opinions on your goal. These don't matter. This is noise. What matters the most is your belief towards your goal - if you doubt it, it ain't your goal. You gotta take advice from the one person who has achieved what you dream of becoming; not from the nine others who are just messing around with their lives. Make the most meaning out of your life. It's your life, you have to make it large. You have to take the driver's seat. You have to take the ownership.

All in all, surround yourlsef with positive people and always keep in mind that it is all about not losing hope.

Ciao!
